# A2 – 3D Printing Lab Week 2

Overhangs in FDM are when unsupported material is bridging a gap in the part. This can cause problems with sagging while printing. The work around is either to change the print orientation to compensate for this or by increasing the part cooling to attempt to harden the filament faster.

Andy: Overhangs in DfAM are similar to overhangs in FDM. One work around is to add supports to the part to support the overhang. Layers in FDM are typically visible on the part but can be made less visible by reducing layer thickness to 0.1mm for example (detailed setting) in order to increase the print resolution and improve the surface finish. The downside is it roughly doubles the print time.
