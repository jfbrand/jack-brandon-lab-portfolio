1. Transforming Coiling Planar Linkages (2026)

<img width="400" height="400" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1f70cb6b-df77-4274-8da5-d1b19d15b132" />

This linkage mechanism works using a four-bar linkage system to coil and uncoil with one degree of motion. It has strings or "tendons" that can be pulled to individually to either coil or uncoil the linkage. It is unique due to it following a cycloidal path when uncoiling from its "root" and an involute path when uncoiling from its "tip". It can also be transformed from a compact coil shape to a rigid truss structure when needed.

Uses: This mechanism could be used for deploying temporary bridges or non-permanent structures, therefore being useful for military or rescue operations. It could also be useful in robotics or prosthetics industries.

Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40648-025-00289-3
